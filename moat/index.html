01 · The framework

Two enterprise blueprints, one moat map.

Your source material — J.P. Morgan's AskDavid multi-agent design and BlackRock's RockAI / Aladdin platform for regulated industries — describe the same shape from two directions. Collapse them and you get four investable layers. The higher the layer, the closer to the trust moat.

Source · J.P. Morgan

AskDavid — the multi-agent approach

  • Supervisor Agent orchestrates, plans, keeps a human in the loop & holds long-term memory.
  • Specialised sub-agents: Structured-Data (NL→SQL/GraphQL), Unstructured-Data, Analytics.
  • A Reflection loop checks accuracy & reliability before answering.
  • Memory + RAG: docs → chunking → indexing → embedding.
Source · BlackRock

RockAI by Aladdin — regulated-industry platform

  • Layered stack: Presentation → Agents → Models → Datasets.
  • Trustworthy AI: evaluation, guardrails, observability.
  • Security, Governance & Compliance spine: RBAC, encryption, SR 26-2 / OCC / EU AI Act / GDPR.
  • Audit & Traceability: monitoring, compliance logging, DR/HA.

02 · The screen

Four tests for a business that compounds.

A durable software franchise doesn't just grow — it grows on top of revenue it never loses, inside systems customers can't cheaply leave. Each company is scored on all four, then blended into a single Moat Score.

Test 01

Trust Moat

Would ripping this out put the customer's security, compliance or data integrity at risk? Trust is the deepest moat in regulated AI.

Weight in score30%
Test 02

Net Revenue Retention

Does the same customer spend more each year? NRR > 110% means growth compounds before a single new logo is won.

Weight in score24%
Test 03

Switching Cost

Data gravity, integrations, workflow and habit. The harder the exit, the more pricing power and the flatter the churn curve.

Weight in score24%
Test 04

Rule of 40

Revenue growth % + free-cash-flow margin % ≥ 40. The discipline test: growth that isn't bought with permanent losses.

Weight in score22%

05 · Methodology & honesty

How the score is built.

The Moat Score

Each company carries four inputs. Retention and Rule of 40 are normalised to 0–100, then blended:

Score = 0.30·TrustMoat + 0.24·Switching + 0.24·NRRₙ + 0.22·R40ₙ

  • NRRₙ maps 95%→0 and 130%→100.
  • R40ₙ maps a Rule-of-40 sum of 0→0 and 80→100.
  • Where NRR isn't disclosed (mega-caps), its weight is redistributed across the other three.

Where a trust moat actually lives

The thesis from your two blueprints: durable value accretes to the upper layers — the data substrate agents reason over, and the security / governance / trust spine that regulated buyers can't operate without. These are the layers with the highest switching cost and the flattest churn.
